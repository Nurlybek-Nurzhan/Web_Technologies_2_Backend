About Fitclub

Transforming lives through fitness since 2020. We're more than a gym - we're a community dedicated to helping you achieve your best self.

Our Story

Founded in 2020 by a group of passionate fitness enthusiasts, Fitclub began as a vision to create more than just another gym. We wanted to build a community where people of all fitness levels could come together, support each other, and achieve their health and wellness goals.

What started as a small neighborhood fitness center has grown into Astana's premier fitness destination, serving over 2,500 members with state-of-the-art facilities, expert trainers, and innovative programs designed to meet every fitness need.

Our journey is built on the belief that fitness should be accessible, enjoyable, and transformative. Every day, we witness incredible transformations - not just physical, but mental and emotional as well.
